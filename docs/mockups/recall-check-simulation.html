Multi-stage recall research — the three indicators in motion

A click-through simulation of the rdw-scamshield-recall-check-v2 skill running on the app's /chat surface (mode skill:recall-check). Smoke test → routed triage → gated verify stages → consolidation. The right rail is the app's UVP: verdict, confidence, and ripeness as three separate, independent indicators — watch how they move independently as evidence lands: the verdict pins early, confidence climbs as records are fetched, and ripeness falls as the remaining value of one more stage is spent.

⚠︎ Illustrative scenario. “Fjordline Smokehouse” is a fictional brand and every finding, date, and citation below is simulated mockup content — not real research output, not a real recall.

Why three lines? A verdict can be 🔴 while confidence is still Medium (snippet-verified) and ripeness is 🟢 (lots left to verify) — or a 🟢 verdict can be low-confidence because coverage is thin. Collapsing them into one score would hide exactly the decision the user has to make: act now, or pay for one more stage?
