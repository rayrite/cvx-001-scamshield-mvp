Video samples

Two MP4 players streamed straight from the service volume with HTTP Range — seek anywhere on the timeline and the browser fetches just that slice (206 Partial Content). Layout and theme follow the central UDL config like every other page.

Placeholders expect demo1.mp4 and demo2.mp4 in the volume's videos/ folder — app/data/videos/ locally, /data/videos/ on Railway (upload with railway volume files upload <file> /videos/<file>). Files must be browser-ready MP4: H.264 + AAC with faststart — see docs/VIDEO-HOSTING-RAILWAY.md.
