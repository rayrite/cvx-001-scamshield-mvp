🗺️ Threat Radar

Where a recall or outbreak actually lands, on a geocode-accurate U.S. map. In production this card is a post-processing stage on recall research: the report's place names are extracted into a location table, geocoded by real geocoders (Census · Nominatim), and pinned from those coordinates — never from a generated illustration. These scenarios render real, cited public data: a vehicle recall, a food recall, and a disease outbreak snapshot.
