How a check runs

The agent never free-wheels: the skill defines the stages, the gauges say when to stop, and you hold the gates.

Describe or show

Paste the email, listing, or company name — or attach screenshots (transcribed verbatim, defanged).

Snap test

A zero-search pass catches decisive tells instantly — real smishing closes in seconds, no sweep needed.

Staged sweep

Triage across regulator, press, court, and registry sources — with citations and dates, favorable findings included.

Your call

Approve the next stage, redirect the research, or consolidate. The final report keeps verdict, confidence, and ripeness separate.
